Leave here what your heart is carrying

One of the convictions that holds this whole devotional together is that grace meets you where you are — not where you think you should be. You don't need elegant words or a perfect faith to ask for prayer. You only need to be honest.

How we care for your request

With honesty, not empty promises

  • It's read with care. Every request you send is read by Lyanette or someone on her direct team — slowly, unhurried, the way you'd read a letter from a friend.
  • It stays private. Your request is never published on the site or shared outside this team, unless you check the box to share it anonymously in our prayer circle.
  • It isn't a promise of a personal reply. Because of the volume of messages, we can't always respond to you individually. But every name, every word written here, is carried in prayer — of that you can be certain.
