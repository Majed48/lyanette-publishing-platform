Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy describes what information the Messages from God to the Heart website ("the site," "we," "us") operated by Lyanette Valiente collects, how it is used, and what choices you have about it. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. What information we collect

We collect information in the following situations:

  • Newsletter form: your email address, when you subscribe to receive weekly reflections.
  • Prayer request form: your name (optional) and the content of your request.
  • General contact form: your name, email address, the subject you select, and the message you write.
  • Shopping cart and orders: items you add to your cart are saved locally in your own device's browser (localStorage), not on a server. This information does not travel to our systems until you complete a purchase.

The site does not currently have a server-hosted user account system: the "My Account" and "Downloads" information you see reflected in your browser is also saved locally on your device, for the purpose of demonstrating the shopping experience.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you share with us only for the purposes it was given:

  • To send you the weekly newsletter, if you subscribed, and to allow you to unsubscribe at any time.
  • To read and hold in prayer the requests you send us, with as much discretion as possible.
  • To respond to your general contact messages — press, collaborations, support, or other topics.
  • To process and deliver your digital purchases, once we integrate an active payment system.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share prayer requests with anyone outside Lyanette Valiente's direct team.

3. Cookies and local storage

This site uses your browser's local storage (localStorage), not advertising-tracking cookies, to remember your preferences as you browse:

  • The contents of your shopping cart.
  • Your order history within the site.
  • Reading preferences in the online reader, such as reading progress, your bookmarks, dark mode, and font size.
  • Your local account name and email, if you created a profile in "My Account."

Your preferred language is no longer stored through a cookie or local preference: it is determined by the site address you visit (for example, pages under /es/ or /en/), so you can change it at any time using the ES / EN selector at the top of the page. All other information remains saved only on your own device and browser; you can delete it at any time from your browser settings. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on this site.

4. Third parties and payment processors

Today this site does not process payments directly: the paperback and Kindle links direct you to Amazon, whose own privacy policy applies to that transaction. When we integrate a payment processor for direct digital purchases (a specific online payment provider that will be named at that time), we will update this policy to name the specific provider and describe what data is shared with it, such as billing information necessary to complete the purchase.

We may in the future use an external email newsletter management service; in that case, your email address would be shared only with that provider for the purpose of sending weekly reflections, and this policy would be updated to reflect it.

5. How long we keep your information

We retain contact messages and prayer requests for as long as needed to respond to them and hold them in prayer. Your newsletter email is retained for as long as you remain subscribed, and is removed from our lists when you unsubscribe. Information stored in localStorage remains only on your device until you delete it or clear your browser.

6. Children

This site is not directed at children under thirteen, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has shared information with us, contact us so we can remove it.

7. Your rights

You may ask us at any time to: access the information we hold about you, correct it, request its deletion, or unsubscribe from the newsletter. To exercise any of these rights, write to us through our contact page, clearly stating your request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, especially as the site grows — for example, when we integrate a payment processor or a server-hosted account system. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or about how we handle your information, write to us from the contact page.
