Why this book

Written for the heart that needs to rest

Lyanette didn't write this devotional from theory, but from her own long nights. Every chapter grows out of one conviction, held in four truths.

01

God's love is constant

It doesn't depend on your good days, and it doesn't withdraw on the hard ones.

02

Hope carries you through

Even when you can't yet see the end of the road.

03

Peace is a daily discipline

Not a one-time event, but a practice for each morning.

04

Grace meets you where you are

Without asking you to arrive anywhere first.

This book is gracefully written and inspired by the Holy Spirit. We are saved by grace and loved by Jesus.
