FAQ

Answers before you ask

Here are the questions readers ask us most, about pricing, formats, shipping, and your account. If you don't find what you're looking for, write to us.

Purchases and pricing

Purchases and pricing

What's the difference between the digital edition and the paperback?

The digital edition ($14.99) includes EPUB and PDF files with instant download from your account — ideal if you want to start reading today. The paperback edition is a printed book, shipped through Amazon, with its own price and delivery time. Both contain exactly the same forty reflections.

Can I buy both editions (Spanish and English) together?

Yes. We offer a bundle with both digital editions — Spanish and English — for $22.99, a savings compared to buying them separately. You can find it in the store or on the book page.

What's the refund policy for digital purchases?

Because digital content is delivered instantly, we offer refunds within the first 7 days after purchase if you haven't yet downloaded the files, or if you had a technical issue we couldn't resolve. Write to us through contact with your order number and we'll be glad to help.

Do discount coupons apply to the Amazon paperback?

No — coupons published on our site (such as newsletter or special promotions) only apply to digital purchases made directly with us. Paperback and Kindle prices on Amazon are governed by Amazon's own promotions.

Digital formats

Digital formats

Should I choose EPUB or PDF?

Both formats are included with your digital purchase, so you don't have to choose. EPUB adapts to your screen size and is ideal for readers like Kindle, Apple Books, or Google Play Books. PDF preserves the exact page design, just as it appears in the print edition — perfect if you prefer to print chapters or read on a computer.

Can I read the book on my phone?

Yes. You can read directly in our online reader from any browser, or download the EPUB to your phone and open it with a reading app such as Apple Books, Google Play Books, or Moon+ Reader. The online reader includes font-size control and dark mode for comfortable nighttime reading.

On how many devices can I use my digital files?

Once downloaded, the EPUB and PDF files are yours for personal use on all your devices — phone, tablet, computer, or e-reader. We simply ask that they not be redistributed or shared publicly, as described in our terms of use.

Is the edition bilingual, or are they two separate books?

They're two independent editions: Messages from God to the Heart in English and Mensajes de Dios del Corazón in Spanish. Each has its own cover and its own file — it isn't one book with both languages mixed together. You can buy whichever you prefer, or both in the bilingual bundle.

Shipping and Amazon

Shipping and Amazon

Who ships the paperback edition?

The paperback and Kindle version are sold and printed on demand through Amazon. This means the shipping, packaging, and customer service for those orders are handled directly by Amazon, following their own delivery times and policies — not ours.

Do you ship internationally?

International shipping availability depends on Amazon and your country of residence. We recommend checking shipping options directly on the product page on Amazon before completing your purchase.

How do I return a printed book I bought on Amazon?

Returns of printed products purchased on Amazon are handled through Amazon's return policy, not ours. You can start a return from your Amazon account under "Your Orders."

Account and downloads

Account and downloads

Can I re-download my files if I lose my phone or computer?

Yes. Your digital files remain available in the Downloads section of your account, so you can re-download them as many times as you need, on any new device.

Do I need to create an account to buy?

You can currently browse the store and the sample reader without an account. To complete a digital purchase and access your downloads afterward, we will ask for some basic details during checkout.

I didn't receive my purchase confirmation email — what do I do?

First check your spam or promotions folder. If it still hasn't appeared after a few minutes, write to us through contact with the email address you used to purchase, and we'll be glad to check your order.
