Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what this site does with cookies and browser storage. If you're looking for broader information about how we handle your data in general, visit our privacy policy.

1. What local storage (localStorage) is

"Local storage," or localStorage, is a feature built into your browser that lets a website save small amounts of data directly on your own device — not on an external server. Think of it as a notepad your browser keeps on your computer or phone to remember your preferences the next time you visit the site. No one else can read that notepad, and we can't see it from our own systems either: it lives only on your device.

This site does not use advertising-tracking or third-party cookies. What we technically use is the browser's localStorage and sessionStorage, which serve a purpose similar to a traditional cookie but stay exclusively on your device.

2. What we store and why

We currently use browser local storage to remember the following, always on your own device:

  • Shopping cart: items you add before completing a purchase.
  • Order history: a local record of your purchases within this site, visible in your account.
  • Reading progress: the chapter or page where you left off in the online reader, along with your saved bookmarks.
  • Reader preferences: the font size you chose and whether you turned on dark mode.
  • Your account name and email: the details you entered when creating your profile in "My Account" on this device.

Your preferred language is no longer stored as a local preference: the site's language is now part of the web address itself you visit (for example, pages under /es/ show Spanish and pages under /en/ show English), so you can change it at any time with the ES / EN selector, without relying on data saved in your browser.

All of the information above is saved and read locally, in the browser you're using right now. Today, none of this is sent to a server: we don't have an externally hosted account system, so this data exists only on your device.

3. What this means for you

  • If you clear your browser's browsing data (cache, cookies, and site storage), these preferences will reset: your cart will empty, your order history, reading progress, and bookmarks will disappear, and you will need to choose your font size and dark mode again.
  • If you visit the site from another browser or device, you won't find the same saved information, because it doesn't travel between devices — it stays only where you created it.
  • You can delete this information manually at any time from your browser's privacy settings, with no need to contact us.

4. Third-party cookies and advertising

This site does not currently use third-party cookies for advertising purposes or cross-site tracking tools. We also have no traffic analytics service (such as Google Analytics or similar) integrated today. If we add any such tool in the future, we will update this policy to clearly explain what is added, for what purpose, and what choices you will have about it.

5. Fonts and external resources

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts to correctly display the brand's visual styles. This type of connection is technical and necessary to display the page's design; we do not use it for advertising-tracking purposes.

6. How to manage or delete this information

The simplest way to delete everything this site has stored in your browser is to clear your browsing data (sometimes called "cookies and site data") from your browser's privacy settings, either for this site specifically or for all sites you visit. Keep in mind that this will also log you out of your local session, empty your cart, and reset your reading preferences.

7. Changes to this policy

If this site begins using third-party cookies, analytics tools, or a server-hosted account system in the future, we will update this page to reflect it clearly. The "last updated" date at the top will always show the most recent version.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this cookie policy or about how local storage works on this site, write to us from the contact page. For more detail about the general handling of your data, see our privacy policy.
