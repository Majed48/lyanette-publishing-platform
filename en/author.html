The Author

Lyanette Valiente

Devotional writer, teacher, and messenger of hope. She writes from her own journey of faith to walk alongside others on theirs.

Biography

A story written slowly

Lyanette Valiente didn't always know she would write a devotional. For years she kept her prayers in private notebooks — words written in the quietest hours, when her heart needed to name what it was living through. It was during one of those seasons of waiting, when answers were slower to come than she would have liked, that she began to notice a pattern: every time she sat down to write out her prayer, something in her grew still. Writing became her way of talking with God, and in time, her way of serving others.

Born and raised in a home of faith, Lyanette learned early that the Christian life isn't free of pain — but it is accompanied. She has walked through loss, doubt, and nights where faith held on more by habit than by certainty. It was in that very honesty, not in easy answers, that she found her voice as a writer: a voice that doesn't minimize suffering, but never stops pointing toward hope.

Today Lyanette writes for those who, like her, are searching for a quiet space in the middle of the noise. Messages from God to the Heart grew out of those private notebooks, rewritten and shared with the conviction that no honest prayer is ever wasted — and that the word of God, read slowly, still has the power to heal.

Faith Journey

From private notebooks to the public page

Lyanette's faith hasn't followed a straight line. There were years of quiet certainty, and years where praying felt like speaking into an empty room. She doesn't hide that second part — she includes it, because she knows it's where most of her readers also spend the greater part of their time.

What held her faith together wasn't quick answers, but a decision made again and again: to keep showing up. To keep praying even when she felt nothing. To keep reading the Word even when today's verse seemed no different from yesterday's. Over the years, that quiet steadiness became the backbone of everything she writes.

“I'm not writing from the top of the mountain. I'm writing from the path, still climbing, telling you what I see from here.”
Lyanette Valiente
Why This Book Was Written

A letter that began as hers alone

Messages from God to the Heart began as something much smaller: handwritten notes after morning prayer, never intended for anyone else to read. Lyanette wrote them to process what she was feeling, so as not to lose what God showed her in those minutes of silence.

It was a close friend, walking through her own difficult season, who asked to read one of those notes. Then she asked for another. And another. Lyanette began to notice that the words holding her together were also holding up other people — and that's where the idea was born to gather them into a devotional, chapter by chapter, so that anyone could find them when they needed them.
