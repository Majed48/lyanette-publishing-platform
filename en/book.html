About this devotional

Messages from God to the Heart

by Lyanette Valiente

Messages from God to the Heart grew out of Lyanette's own long nights — out of the questions she brought to God when silence seemed like the only answer. This devotional gathers ten chapters written like letters: short, honest, and rooted in Scripture, meant to be read slowly, one day at a time.

You won't find quick formulas or empty promises here. You'll find a companion who has walked through weariness, waiting, and doubt, and who now reaches out with the same tenderness with which God draws near to a hurting heart.

Each chapter weaves together a personal reflection, a central verse, and space for prayer — so this book doesn't just rest in your hands, but reaches all the way to your heart.
