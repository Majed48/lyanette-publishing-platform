## 1. Creative treatment

**Title:** We Bake Smiles — From Dough to Table

**The idea in one line:** Every manaeesh at Zaatar Factory travels the same short, honest road — dough, zaatar, fire, hands, a smile — and we film that road so closely you can smell it.

**Tone:** Quiet morning becomes a warm, full room. The film starts nearly silent (flour, breath of the oven) and ends with a room full of people. The emotional arc is *solitude of craft → generosity of sharing*.

**Visual signature (what makes it ownable):**
1. **The Zaatar Shine.** Every hero item gets one shot where raking window light catches olive oil on zaatar. This is the brand's recurring "logo shot" — reused across every series.
2. **The Hand-Off.** The baker's floured hands pass food to a customer's hands. Hands, not faces, carry the story — more authentic, and far more reliable in AI generation.
3. **Green & gold live in graphics only.** The footage stays natural (golden bread, green zaatar, white cheese). Brand color appears in type, lower thirds, and the end card.

**What we are deliberately NOT doing:** no menu slideshow, no logo opening, no stock "happy family laughing at camera," no Arabic-music clichés, no prices, no AI-generated text.

## 7. Music direction

- **Brief:** 40s modern cinematic bed, ~84 BPM, D Dorian. Bars 1–4 near-silence (pad + room). Enters at 0:13 with a single plucked oud motif and frame-drum/riq ghost notes; warm upright-bass pulse from 0:18; gentle swell into 0:30–0:34; resolves on the end card.
- **Avoid:** belly-dance rhythms, maqam "tourist" runs, big trailer drums, ukulele-corporate.
- **Source:** generate 3 candidates (ElevenLabs music or Higgsfield audio), or license from a library (Artlist/Musicbed). **Check commercial-use rights in the license before publishing on paid ads.**
- **Mix rule:** music sits −20 to −24 LUFS-short under VO; ducks −6dB under each food sound hero (tear, sizzle, crunch).

## 16. Risk list (ranked)

1. **AI food realism — highest risk.** Cheese stretch, egg yolk, and tearing bread are where AI breaks (rubber cheese, liquid that morphs). Mitigation: keep stretches short (<1s), cut on action, reject anything rubbery, have real phone footage as fallback for S5.
2. **The AI bakery isn't their bakery.** A customer walks in and sees a different room. Mitigation: real interior photos as references; keep wide interior shots minimal; hands and food carry the film.
3. **Baker consistency across 8+ shots.** Faces drift between generations. Mitigation: face appears in only 2 shots (S3-B, soft focus) — everything else is hands and apron, which hold continuity far better.
4. **Hands.** Extra fingers/merging in dough work. Mitigation: start frames with clear hand poses; reject any hand error at draft stage.
5. **Manaeesh read as pizza.** Mitigation: explicit shape/thickness/zaatar-color language + negative "no pizza"; reference the real menu photos.
6. **Child in an ad.** AI child faces trigger more scrutiny and can feel uncanny. Mitigation: hand + partial profile, or swap for an adult.
7. **Credits overrun.** Mitigation: drafts first, 3-shot test gate, budget projection.
8. **Music licensing.** Generated or library music must be cleared for paid ads.
9. **Handle typo** (@zaatarfactory vs @zatarfactory) — a wrong handle on a CTA wastes the whole ad.
10. **Platform UI covers the CTA.** Mitigation: end card built inside TikTok/IG safe zones (top 220px, bottom 480px, right 160px kept clear of key info).

## 17. QA criteria (pass/fail per shot and per deliverable)

**Per shot (reject on any fail):** food looks real and appetizing · hands anatomically correct · same bench/oven/apron/board as references · no text, signage, or logos in footage · no morphing between frames · steam/oil/cheese obey physics · camera move serves the food · doesn't look like stock.

**Per deliverable:** hook lands in first 1–2s · reads without sound (captions) · better with sound · VO natural, not announcer · music never masks food sounds · end card ≥5s on hero, ≥3s on cutdowns · every CTA string matches the locked table above, character-for-character · no prices · safe-zones respected · exports: 1080×1920 H.264 high, 48kHz, −14 LUFS; 1080×1350; 1920×1080.
